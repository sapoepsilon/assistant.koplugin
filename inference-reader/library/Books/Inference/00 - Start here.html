Ask the AI

  • Long-press a word, or long-press and drag over a passage.
  • Tap Explain, Simplify, Why it matters or Define terms.
  • Ask AI… for your own question about the selection.
  • Hold the bottom-left corner: explain the whole page.
  • Hold the bottom-right corner: ask the AI anything.

Quick menu

Tap the top-left corner. It has: explain page, ask AI, switch AI provider (LAN / Tailscale), layouts, figures in true colours, night mode, contents, previous / next paper, library.

Layouts (PDF)

  • Content width, scroll: default on the inner screen. Swipe up and down.
  • One column at a time: best on the cover screen. Tap right / left to move. Chosen automatically when the phone is folded.
  • Whole page: for figures and tables. Pinch to zoom.
  • Figures in true colours: un-inverts the page so plots look right; tap again for dark.

Other gestures

  • Tap the bottom-left corner: night mode on/off.
  • Tap the top-right corner: bookmark.
  • Two-finger swipe right: contents. Two-finger swipe left: bookmarks.
  • Draw →←→: next paper. Draw ←→←: previous paper.
  • Tap the top of the screen: menu. Tap the bottom: page settings.

Library

Papers live in Books/Inference (numbered in reading order) and Books/Inference/DeepSeek. Drop new PDFs into either folder; they also show up under Collections.
